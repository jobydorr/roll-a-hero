# Aramil

## What you know

Your family has always said the Folding was wrong. Folding the world goes against the way things are meant to be, and you grew up hearing that. Most people you meet think the Folding was the best thing that ever happened. Your family never did.

When you found out the Surveyors rule the whole world, it made you angry. It still does.

## Your memory from the Fold

When you stepped into the Fold, a memory hit you, sharp and real. Screaming. Everything going wrong at once. Running. Orcs attacking, and your family being taken.

Here's the strange part. You didn't know you had this memory until that moment. And orcs are one of the things that were shut away in the bad time, centuries ago. Most people have never seen one outside a story.

## Things to think about

- Where was your family when this happened, and where are they now?
- If the monsters are shut away, how did orcs get to your family?
- The people painting THE FOLDWRIGHT LIVES are against the Surveyors too. Are they on your side?
