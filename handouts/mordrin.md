# Mordrin

*For Brandon only.*

## What you know

Your family runs the Gilded Wheel Trading Company, one of the great trading houses the Ministry releases sunsalt to. The Wheel sells it on and writes salt-notes against it, and like every house it has more notes out than salt in its vaults. Your grandfather backed the Surveyors early, and that's part of why the Wheel is where it is.

You trained at one of the wizards' colleges. To a scholar of magic, the Foldwright is a name from the history of your craft: a great and accomplished mage. You don't put much stock in his being alive.

The Wheel got you the liaison posting a couple of months ago, because it wants to know what fortune is waiting inside the creases. Your family thinks you're out scouting business ventures.

## Your secret

You're Red Flag. Your cell is small and doesn't know any other cell. You report to one person and know one other member; who they are is still yours to decide. You'll report on this party, but you want to see what they can do before you recommend them. You're trying to help Red Flag without hurting the Wheel or getting it blamed.

## Your memory from the Fold

A pet lizard you kept at wizard school. You have no idea what happened to it. It's a small thing, and it shook you anyway, because you didn't know the memory was missing until it came back. So what else is missing?

## Things to think about

- The Wheel's salt-notes outnumber its salt. What happens to your family if people find out?
- Wenna has held her post for nearly two years. What would Red Flag want to know about her?
- The salt-licker's instrument is in your pack, and you can't make it work. Who could tell you how?
