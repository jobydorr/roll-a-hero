# Century

## What you know

You once served someone great: a leader who was powerful, and good, and is now dead. Since then you've wandered, looking for something worth doing with yourself.

## Your memory from the Fold

You were a boy, playing chase with your friends out in the wild. Running through the trees, laughing, trying not to be "it." It's a small, happy memory, and you didn't know you'd lost it until it came back.

## Things to think about

- Who were those friends, and where are they now?
- The leader you served: who killed them, and why?
- As a bard you know a great many old songs. Some of them have always felt like they were missing a verse.
