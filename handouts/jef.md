# Jef

## Your memory from the Fold

When you stepped into the Fold, a memory hit you that you didn't know you had.

You were out training in the woods when something huge flew overhead. Lightning struck. Black smoke rose from the direction of home. You ran back and found two dragons fighting over your village, one black and one blue, while trolls and goblins carried off things and people. Your father was a warrior, and he fought. The lightning got him. You ran into the woods, and you were the last one left.

You had two brothers and a sister.

Dragons haven't been seen since the bad time, centuries ago. But trolls and goblins carrying people off is exactly what you saw happen at Lastlight.

## Things to think about

- The goblins took people from your village. Were your brothers and sister taken, or lost some other way?
- Why were two dragons fighting each other?
- Who was training you in the woods?
