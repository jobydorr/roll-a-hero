# Sam

## What you know

You don't remember your life. Not your home, not your family, not how you came to be here. You still know the world, everything in the shared pages, and your hands still know your work: tracking, the bow, the wild. Worm, your weasel, sticks close to you as if he's known you forever.

A few things you just know, the way your hands know how to string a bow, without knowing how you know them. Your people were wanderers, and they followed old routes across the world. The Folding cut those routes apart.

## The one memory you have

A wall at night. Everything is dark except for what looks like firelight moving on the wall. You're sure this memory is yours. It's the only thing about yourself you're sure of.

## What Drew knows about Sam

This part is for you, the player, not for Sam. Losing his memory hasn't erased anything about who Sam is. Everything you came to the table with is still true and still yours:

- Sam was close to retiring. He was world-weary and done with the life he'd led, and this journey was meant to be one last hurrah before he moved on.
- He was only passing through.
- His people were wanderers whose old routes the Folding broke apart.
- Worm is his.

Sam can't remember any of that right now, but you can, and you can let it show in how you play him: in his instincts, his habits, and what he's drawn to or uneasy around. Pieces of it may come back to him along the way.

## Things to think about

- What was burning?
- Which side of the wall were you on?
- Worm seems to know things about you. What does he do that surprises you?
