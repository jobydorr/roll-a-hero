## 1. The Arc of Act One

The act runs from the Hearthspire ceremony to the turn — Ellum Wick's field book beside Tobin's filings, and a dark window at the end of the trail. The beats, seeded from `CAMPAIGN.md` §6 and to be expanded here to session grain:

1. **The Last Draw and the Hearthspire.** The ceremony, the disaster, the taken, Wenna in the dust. Session one, and it doubles as session zero (see §2).
2. **The wreck.** Green Fields invokes the right of search; the party enters the dead Hearthspire with a clerk attached. The sealed door, the wagon-ruts, the crushed garland, the patched tear.
3. **The first lost place.** Partly gray, under a sky that has never held a lamp. Ellum's blaze-marks. The first taken recovered — and the first things the bible has been saving: the Compass Spirit, the Foldwright glimpsed in the creases.
4. **Home between doors.** The city between ventures: Tobin's healing surfaces (before his filings do), the first filing Wenna wasn't told about, the gray arithmetic beginning to itch.
5. **The satchel.** Ellum's cached field book. Routes assigned from above; an expedition steered away from every place worth searching; a page that names a town with a dark window.
6. **The turn.** Tobin's year of filings laid beside Ellum's pages. The dark window found. A Green Fields officer comes undone along a crease that never lined up. Red Flag inherits the party; Tobin takes both field books and goes.

*The Act One clock (bible §6): breaches and foldquakes worsen this season, where the heroes live, blamed on the wrong name. Track it in §6 of this file.*

### Scene 1 — The ceremony *(frame only; set and setting under construction)*

The canonical skeleton is `CAMPAIGN.md` §6, The Opening — the Last Draw and the Hearthspire: the extinguishing of the Cinder Draw, the honored crews and the white wagons, the folding-away of the gray, the lighting, the tear, the falling clock tower, the fight, Wenna, the two Surveyors writing faces. What this file must add before it is runnable: the physical ground (where the ceremony sits relative to the wall, the Draw, and the tower), the sensory register of the day, the crowd, the staging of the three beats, the fight map, and each hero's spotlight moment.

**Session one doubles as session zero.** The five instruments are in `CAMPAIGN.md` §6 (Session One Is Session Zero). The reason-for-coming is handled by the homework protocol below.

**The homework protocol (the reason for coming).**

- At the top of the session, deliver the line in Joby's words: *each of you has your reason for being here today — and I'll leave it to you to decide what that reason is.* Say explicitly that nobody has to answer now.
- The ask is split by audience. The adults get it open-ended, as true homework. The kids get a reason card — three or four concrete seeds, plus *or make up your own*:
  1. Someone you know is among the honored crews being retired today.
  2. You are looking for someone, and crowds are where you look.
  3. Your family fought in the war, and came to see what the peace bought.
  4. You came to work the crowd — perform, sell, compete, impress.
- The due date lives in the fiction: Wenna's questions in the dust are the collection point, so the homework is returned in character, to a recruiter whose job is asking. Stragglers have until session two.
- Every answer is canonized by saying it back, written into §7 of this file, and must visibly surface in the world within two sessions — an answer that vanishes teaches the table that answers vanish. Once spoken, a reason is a promise.
- No answer is wrong. A hero who remembers something the world has forgotten is one of the haunted, which is a gift; a hero who came to buy a sword is a hero whose sword-seller can know things. Attachment is the DM's work, not the player's.

## 4. The Cast on Stage

*Quick sheets for running, one paragraph each — the full files live in `CAMPAIGN.md` §4. Want first; everything else as the role deserves.*

- **Wenna Ash** — wants the taken found and her movement believed. Sincere to the bone; asks questions like the recruiter she is. Session one: she works the crowd before the lighting, does not run during the fight, and conducts the interview in the dust.
- **Tobin Rell** — wants to do his job well and to not be noticed doing the other thing. Assigned to the party by evening of session one; healing surfaces at the first bad fight; ashen twice over afterward.
- **Hettie Sarn** — wants to be proven wrong and never is. Session one: the old woman at the rope line who does not clap. She is seen, not met. Nothing else yet — her approach is an open question (bible §8 Q12).
- **The two Surveyors at the edge** — want an orderly ceremony and a complete record. They fight with terrible efficiency, patch the tear, bow, and resume writing. The party's first sight of the paper men; unnamed for now.
