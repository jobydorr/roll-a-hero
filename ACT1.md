# Act One — The Staging Ground

*The runbook for building and running Act One at the table. `CAMPAIGN.md` is the bible and holds what is true; this file holds what happens — scenes, encounters, cast sheets, props, clocks, and the running state of play. Where the two disagree, the bible wins and this file is stale — **except** where §8 of this file lists a canon delta the bible has not absorbed yet.*

*Created 2026-08-09; Scene 1 drafted the same day from the Lastlight brainstorm and LOCKED 2026-08-10 (Finch at the chamber, the tear down the tower's face, the Rectors). Sections mirror the DM OS document types (beat, scene, encounter, npc) so finished material can move into `campaign.js` without reshaping. Narrative passages follow `STYLE.md`; table-facing blocks — stat lines, read-alouds, checklists, menus — are working tools and exempt.*

*Act One in one line, from the bible: the rebellion is on a leash, and the hands on the leash are not human.*

## 1. The Arc of Act One

The act runs from the Hearthspire ceremony to the turn — Ellum Wick's field book beside Tobin's filings, and a dark window at the end of the trail. The beats, seeded from `CAMPAIGN.md` §6 and expanded here to session grain:

1. **Lastlight.** The ceremony, the disaster, the captures, the interview. Session one, and it doubles as session zero (see Scene 1).
2. **The transition.** Between the fight and the first venture: how the party ends up going in. **Shape undecided — candidates in Scene 2.**
3. **The way in.** The order opens the ground at the Hearthspire's foot and holds it while the party steps through. The tower itself is never entered.
4. **The first lost place.** Partly gray, under a sky that has never held a lamp. Ellum's blaze-marks. The Lastlight captives recovered — Finch among them, or deeper. The bible's saved cards: the Compass Spirit, the Foldwright glimpsed in the creases.
5. **Home between doors.** Tobin's healing surfaces (before his filings do), the first filing Wenna wasn't told about, the gray arithmetic beginning to itch.
6. **The satchel.** Ellum's cached field book. Routes assigned from above; a page that names a town with a dark window.
7. **The turn.** Tobin's filings beside Ellum's pages; the dark window; a Green Fields officer coming undone along a crease that never lined up. Red Flag inherits the party; Tobin takes both field books and goes.

*The Act One clock (bible §6): breaches and foldquakes worsen this season, where the heroes live, blamed on the wrong name. Track in §6 of this file.*

### The transition collection *(CHOSEN 2026-08-10; unbuilt — Joby has alteration ideas pending for all three)*

The chosen collection, in Joby's words "the collection for now" — each shape is settled as *present*, and none is settled in its details:

1. **The Badge.** The Registry summons the party — and the interview is glowing: the ministry offers them positions as licensed breach-auxiliaries. One hidden bird ends up with the party, quietly trying to unfold itself and drift toward the tear site. **Joby has alteration ideas for this one specifically — do not build it further until they land.**

## 3. Encounters

*Every fight follows the minimum viable rule: one terrain feature someone can interact with, and one moment designed for a specific hero. Kid-simple stat blocks; complexity budget of three to five turns.*

**The table (the standing tuning reference, per Joby 2026-08-10):** four level-3 heroes — a bard, a fighter, and two rangers (the live `dungeon-dads` party). Level 3 is what the builder produces; every encounter in this campaign is tuned for these four. If someone misses a session, the DM lever is to quietly shed one snatcher per empty chair, not to retune.

**Monster policy (settled 2026-08-09).** What comes through tears is the classic bestiary — the monsters of the bad time, sealed at the Folding, recognizable from every grandmother's story. Mechanically: SRD-licensed creatures (`bestiary.js` pipeline) reskinned with folded-space flavor — they arrive sideways, unfold into daylight, and *take captives*; for product-identity classics, original analogues with our own names and wholly original prose (book text never enters the repo). Side effect worth playing: a breach full of storybook monsters *confirms* the ministry's story in the crowd's eyes — the Fold still protects.

**Encounter 1 needs before it runs (the current build queue):** a small monster family for the tear — one minion type with a capture move (grab, then drag toward the tear; rescuing the grabbed is the fight's real objective), one heavier thing, and a scripted opener (the first monster takes Finch before initiative is rolled); terrain features from the fairground (grandstands, fallen clockwork, grain-lanterns, the scar-line); each hero's staged save and staged loss; and the Rector display beats written as read-aloud spectacle, not stat blocks.

- **The Rectors** — the order's ritual grade, pleated into fans and crowns; they lead the ceremony, close the canyon, and fight the disaster as pure spectacle (no dice, never in the party's corner). - **The bird-folder** — the Surveyor at the rope line folding toys that flap, smile a half-second late; among the face-writers by scene's end. Recurs if the Toy Recall shape is chosen. *(Unnamed.)*
- **The two Surveyors at the edge** — want an orderly ceremony and a complete record. The party's first sight of the paper men.
